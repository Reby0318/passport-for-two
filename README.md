# Passport for Two

An anime-style travel comic. Two chibi sprites, drawn from a Seattle waterfront selfie, travel to 100 countries in traditional dress, from a kimono in Kyoto to a feather headdress in Papua New Guinea.

- **Live site:** https://reby0318.github.io/passport-for-two/
- **Also on Claude:** https://claude.ai/artifact/FJWiFZ5UAmj1P2YSoApeYZ

## What's inside

- A character sheet with four looks: Seattle Casual, Black Tie, Cozy Winter and Beach Day
- A prologue in Seattle, 100 country panels and a finale
- A landmark scene, outfits for both characters and speech bubbles in every panel
- Tap a panel to make the couple jump; **Shuffle poses** re-poses everyone

## How it's built

It's a single `index.html` with no build step and no images. The characters, outfits and backgrounds are SVG drawn by plain JavaScript, and the fonts come from Google Fonts. To run it locally, open `index.html` in a browser.
